# Prompts para renders fotorrealistas (Gemini)

Pegar cada prompt tal cual en Gemini y pedir la imagen. Formato vertical 4:5. Si sale algo que no corresponde (más pisos, otra distribución), pedir: "mantené una sola planta, 8 m de frente, sin cambiar los materiales".

Reglas comunes para todos: render arquitectónico fotorrealista estilo V-Ray/Lumion, luz de atardecer cálida, cielo despejado, vegetación subtropical de Paraguay (lapachos, palmeras pindó, césped), sin personas, sin texto ni logos, sin marcas de agua, sin autos de marcas reconocibles.

---

## 1. Casa Brisa 58

Photorealistic architectural render, vertical 4:5, eye-level three-quarter front view at golden hour. A compact modern single-story house, 8 m wide and 7 m deep, on a 10x12 m urban lot in Paraguay. Left volume: white smooth plaster box 3 m high with a thin white flat concrete slab cantilevering 2 m over a front porch, supported by one slim black steel column; under the porch, an entrance door clad in vertical natural wood slats, a perforated exposed-brick lattice panel (terracotta brick screen) beside the door, and a large black-framed sliding glass door. Right volume: slightly taller box (3.4 m) finished in dark graphite render, with a horizontal black-framed window partially covered by vertical wooden louvers. Flat roofs with thin parapets, no visible roof tiles. Warm recessed downlights under the slab, wall sconces, clean concrete path, low ornamental grasses, green lawn, a pink lapacho tree in the background. Minimalist, contemporary, high-end real estate photography style. No people, no text.

## 2. Casa Ñandutí 92

Photorealistic architectural render, vertical 4:5, eye-level three-quarter front view at dusk. A modern single-story family house, 11 m wide, in Paraguay. On the left, an integrated carport under a long thin white cantilevered concrete slab, with one SUV-shaped generic white car. Center: a double-height white volume with a full-height black-framed glass wall showing a warmly lit living room, and a wood-slat entrance door. Right: bedroom wing clad in exposed red brick (ladrillo visto) with two black-framed windows, one covered by vertical wooden louvers. A board-formed concrete fin wall separates the volumes. Columns clad in grey stone, warm recessed lighting under the slabs, wall sconces, small front garden with palms and grasses, concrete driveway. Contemporary, elegant, real estate photography style. No people, no text.

## 3. Dúplex Dúo 70

Photorealistic architectural render, vertical 4:5, eye-level front view at golden hour. A pair of mirrored modern two-story townhouses (duplex), total width 11.6 m, on a 12x30 m lot in Paraguay. Ground floor: white plaster with large black-framed sliding glass doors and wood-slat entrance doors at each end, each unit with its own carport space in front. Upper floor: dark graphite cantilevered box projecting slightly over the ground floor, each unit with a large black-framed window behind a frameless glass balcony railing and a panel of vertical wooden slats. A central dividing wall made of perforated exposed-brick lattice rises the full height between the two units. Flat roof with thin parapet. Warm interior lights, small front gardens with grasses, concrete paving. Clean, contemporary real estate photography. No people, no text.

## 4. Quinta Lapacho

Photorealistic architectural render, vertical 4:5, wide three-quarter view at sunset. A modern country weekend house (quinta) in Paraguay: a closed volume on the left in board-formed concrete and dark graphite with a black-framed sliding glass door and a wood-slat screen; on the right a large open-air covered terrace (quincho) 8 m wide under one long thin flat roof with a warm wooden slat ceiling, slim black steel columns, a built-in exposed-brick barbecue grill with chimney, a long wooden dining table with chairs and a kitchen counter. In front, a 6x3 m rectangular swimming pool with light stone deck, sun loungers, lush lawn, a blooming pink lapacho tree and pindó palms. Warm lights under the roof, relaxed luxury atmosphere, real estate photography style. No people, no text.
